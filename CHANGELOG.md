# Changelog

## Questfund 0.4.0

Release for **World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1**.

- Added a **Questlog** view for active quests, including quests with no related item in your bags or bank.
- View known quest givers, objectives, and turn-in NPCs or objects for regular quests. The world map marks the quest's known locations in the current area. Select **Verfolgen** to track one location on the minimap.
- Added map locations for creature, object, and exploration objectives, plus known drop sources for item objectives. If the index has no objective location, Questfund can use a waypoint supplied by the active quest log.
- Extended Horde and Alliance filtering to regular quests, quest givers, and turn-in NPCs. Hostile targets still appear for combat and loot objectives.
- Replaced the small tracking close icon with a larger **Stopp** button and dedicated click area. You can also end tracking with `/questfund stop`.

## Questfund 0.3.0

First public release for **World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1**.

- Scan your bags and personal bank for quest items, quest starters, and known repeatable turn-in items. Filter items by whether they are linked to a quest in the current log.
- Show turn-in NPCs or objects, zones, coordinates, required quantities, and missing items. See how many items to retrieve from the bank and when its contents were last scanned.
- Filter repeatable turn-ins by quest and destination faction so Horde and Alliance see their own options alongside shared turn-ins.
- Click coordinates to open the world map with the selected destination marked. Use **Verfolgen** to start minimap tracking directly and keep the compass direction and straight-line distance visible with the world map closed.
- Click the minimap marker or tracker for a compact zone map. The marker moves to the minimap edge when the destination is beyond the visible area.
- Open the German-language interface with `/questfund` or the optional Titan Panel launcher.
