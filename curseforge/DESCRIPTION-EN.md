# Questfund

Find quest items in your bags and bank, check what you still need for repeatable turn-ins, and track the right NPC on the map.

Questfund is built for **World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1**. Open the German-language interface with `/questfund`, or use the optional **Questfund** launcher in Titan Panel.

### Find quest items you already own

Scan your backpack, equipped bags, and personal bank. Filter for items linked to active quests, items without a matching quest in your log, quest starters, unknown quest links, or repeatable turn-ins. Select an item to see its bag or bank slot and stack size.

Open your bank once to scan it. Questfund uses the last recorded bank contents while it is closed and shows when they were recorded.

### Plan repeatable turn-ins

Select **Wiederholbar** to see known repeatable turn-ins for your items. Questfund lists the NPC or object, zone, coordinates, and quantity per hand-in where available. It separates what you carry, what you can retrieve from the bank, and how many items you are still missing. Use **Weitere Materialien** to check other known requirements for the same turn-in.

Questfund checks both the quest's faction restrictions and the destination's faction data. Alliance and Horde get their respective turn-ins alongside shared destinations.

### Follow a destination in-game

Click the coordinates to mark the destination on the world map. Select **Verfolgen** to start tracking directly on the minimap and see the compass direction and straight-line distance in yards. The minimap marker moves to the edge when the destination is outside its visible area. Click the minimap marker or the tracker to open a small zone map.

Questfund uses the live quest log when it can identify the required item there and uses bundled reference values for other known turn-ins. Reputation, prerequisites, and server updates can affect availability.
